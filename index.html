About Veloris Global

A trading house built for the modern gemstone market.

We move precious stones, gemstones, minerals and jewelry between the people who find them and the people who need them — wholesalers, retailers, manufacturers and independent buyers across the world.

Every parcel that passes through Veloris Global is checked against origin, grade and documentation before it ships. We don't just broker deals; we hold relationships with cutters and mine operators that keep our pipeline consistent, season after season.

  • Direct sourcing from mining and cutting partners
  • Import / export documentation handled end-to-end
  • Bulk, parcel and single-stone trading available
  • Logistics coordination across air, sea and bonded freight

Sourced across every category that matters.

Precious Stones

Diamond, ruby, sapphire and emerald — graded, certified where required, and traded in parcel or single-stone lots.

Gemstones & Semi-Precious

Tourmaline, amethyst, topaz, garnet, peridot, aquamarine and beyond, sourced direct from cutting houses.

03

Fine Jewelry

Wholesale Lots

Bulk parcels for retailers and distributors, priced by weight, grade and origin.

Digital & Retail Trade

E-commerce and marketplace distribution for buyers who trade in smaller, curated volumes.

Services
